PhD Student · UW–Madison

Songxi Yang

I study satellite remote sensing and machine learning. My work centers on HLS time-series analysis, uncertainty-aware models, and the effect of spatial aggregation choices on prediction accuracy, grounded in MAUP theory.

I am a member of the Spatial Computing and Data Mining Lab at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, advised by Prof. Qunying Huang. I also hold a minor in Computer Science.
